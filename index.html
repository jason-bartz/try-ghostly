No “Ghostly Notetaker” here

Both sides, in one thread

Your microphone and the call’s audio are captured together and written into a floating panel you can read mid-call — as blocks of speech, broken where someone actually stopped talking, so it reads like a conversation rather than a log.

“Where were we?”

One shortcut and you get what's being discussed right now, what you missed, the decisions made, anything asked of you, and one line you could say to rejoin — without looking absent.

Starts when you say so

Ghostly can notice a call by checking whether a conferencing app is really using your mic — then either asks, or runs a countdown you can cancel. Auto-connect is off by default, and you can exclude anything named 1:1, therapy, or interview.

Stays on your Mac

Transcripts are never written to the log file or included in a diagnostics bundle, and are deleted automatically after the retention period you pick. Summaries can run on-device with Apple Intelligence, through your own provider, or with no AI at all.

Capturing the other participants needs macOS 14.2 or later. Below that, Ghostly transcribes your side only — and tells you so.

Open Mic

Stop pressing keys altogether.

Arm the mic once and it stays open. Every pause closes a segment, transcribes it, and drops it where your cursor is — no shortcut per sentence, no reaching back to the keyboard between thoughts.

End a segment with your submit phrase and Ghostly strips the phrase and presses the key for you. Say "send it" and the message is gone.

Off by default. An open microphone is a real trade, so it's yours to make — close it with the shortcut or the menu bar whenever you step away.

Features

What Ghostly does

On-device transcription

Whisper in four sizes — Small (466 MB), Turbo (809 MB), Medium (1.5 GB), Large-v3 (3.1 GB) — GPU-accelerated via Metal. Swap in Parakeet or Moonshine if you'd rather. Same UI.

One shortcut, any app

Push-to-talk or toggle. Pastes into whatever has focus — Slack, Xcode, Mail, Figma, Cursor, the URL bar.

Meeting Mode Max

Live transcripts of your calls in a floating panel — your mic and the call's audio, on one timeline, with a notepad underneath. Nothing joins the meeting; no screen recording permission needed.

"Where were we?"

Catch up mid-call: what's being discussed now, what you missed, the decisions, anything asked of you, and one line you could say to rejoin.

Open Mic

Hands-free dictation. Leave the mic armed and every pause closes a segment. Finish with "send it" and Ghostly strips the phrase and presses ⏎.

Notes & meeting library

Every dictation and every meeting saved on-device — searchable, taggable, groupable by day or app, and exportable as Markdown, text, or JSON.

AI cleanup, no key required

Apple Intelligence runs it on-device, free, with nothing to set up. Or point it at OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, OpenRouter, Cerebras, Z.AI or Ollama with your own key — only the transcript is sent, never audio.

Per-app writing styles

A different prompt and vocabulary per app, matched on the real bundle identifier. Casual in Slack, formal in Mail, verbatim in your editor.

Verbatim dictation

A separate shortcut that transcribes exactly what you said with no rewriting at all — the escape hatch for quotes, code, names, and anything an AI shouldn't touch.

Edit what you just said

Say "make it shorter" or "change Monday to Tuesday" — or use the quick chips: shorten, lengthen, fix grammar, rephrase. Rewritten in place.

Screenshot + dictate

Captures your screen alongside your question and stages both, ready to drop into any AI chat that takes images. "Why is this build failing?" — error already attached.

Voice commands & "scratch that"

Map spoken phrases to keystrokes — "approve" sends ⏎, "reject" sends ⎋. Say "scratch that" mid-sentence and everything before it drops.

Plus a personal dictionary for the names, products, and jargon it keeps getting wrong — including how they sound versus how they're spelled.

FAQ

Questions

Does my audio leave my Mac?▾

No. Transcription runs on-device via Whisper. If you enable optional AI cleanup with a cloud provider, only the resulting text is sent — never audio. Apple Intelligence keeps cleanup on-device too.

Is it free?▾

Yes, and the free tier is uncapped — unlimited dictation, all 16 transcription models, Open Mic, per-app profiles, custom vocabulary, screenshot capture, on as many Macs as you own. Cleanup and voice editing work out of the box on Apple Intelligence, on-device and with no key, and you can point them at your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq or Ollama key instead. Ghostly Max ($12/month, or $108/year) is the optional paid tier: the latest Anthropic models picked per job with no key to manage, plus Meeting Mode, Ask, vocabulary learning and encrypted sync. See what's included.

Do I need Max to use the AI features?▾

Not for dictation. Cleanup and voice editing both work on the free tier, and on Apple Silicon they need no key and no network at all — Apple Intelligence is built in and runs on the Neural Engine. If you would rather use a cloud model, bring a key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Groq or OpenRouter, or point it at Ollama. Meeting Mode is part of Max, along with Ask, vocabulary learning and encrypted sync.

If I subscribe to Max, does my audio get uploaded?▾

No. Transcription always runs on your Mac, on every tier. Max sends the resulting text to a model for cleanup or summarising — the same thing that happens on the free tier with your own key, just without you holding the key. Your microphone audio is never uploaded, and per-app rules let you mark apps as local-only so nothing leaves at all.

Does Ghostly join my meetings like a notetaker bot?▾

No. Meeting Mode captures your microphone and the call’s audio locally through a macOS process tap, so nothing joins the call and no bot appears in the participant list. Transcripts stay on your Mac, are never written to logs or diagnostics bundles, and are deleted automatically after the retention period you choose. Capturing the other participants needs macOS 14.2 or later; below that Ghostly transcribes your side only and says so.

What is “Where were we?”▾

A shortcut that summarises the meeting you’re in: what’s being discussed right now, what you missed, the decisions made, anything asked of you, and one line you could say to rejoin the conversation. Summaries can be generated on-device with Apple Intelligence, through your own AI provider, or extractively with no AI at all.

What is Open Mic?▾

A hands-free mode. Instead of holding a shortcut per sentence, you leave the mic open and Ghostly transcribes each utterance when you pause. End a segment with your submit phrase — “send it” by default — and Ghostly strips the phrase and presses Enter for you. It’s off by default, and the mic stays open only while you have it armed.

What are the system requirements?▾

macOS 12 or later on Apple Silicon — any Mac with an M1 chip or later. Intel Macs are not supported. Microphone and accessibility permissions required.

Why Apple Silicon only?▾

Ghostly runs every AI model on-device — Whisper transcription via Metal, and optional Apple Intelligence cleanup via the Neural Engine. Real-time local dictation needs the unified memory and ML accelerators that only Apple Silicon has. Supporting Intel would mean either unusable latency or shipping your audio to a server — a trade-off we won't make.

Does it work offline?▾

Yes. After the first-run model download, dictation works offline. Optional AI cleanup needs a network connection unless you pick Apple Intelligence.

Windows, Linux, or Intel Mac?▾

No. Ghostly is Apple Silicon only — M1 or later. Intel Macs, Windows, and Linux are not supported.
